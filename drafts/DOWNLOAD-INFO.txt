================================================================================
                      CENTRAL THESIS
================================================================================

"The future of software engineering belongs to organisations that treat
specifications, not source code, as their primary intellectual asset."

Why this matters:
1. RFCs proved it works for 50 years (internet architecture)
2. AI makes it economical now (implementation takes minutes)
3. Knowledge outlives technology (preserve business logic, not code)

Three key insights:
✓ Specifications are durable; implementations are transient
✓ Different technologies can implement the same specification
✓ When specs are clear, AI produces consistent, high-quality code
