KEY CONCEPTS
================================================================================

The Specification Stack:
1. Vision - Why does this system exist?
2. Requirements - What observable behaviour is needed?
3. Use Cases - What is the complete user interaction?
4. Domain Model - What entities and relationships exist?
5. Architecture - What structural constraints guide implementation?
6. Skills - How does this organisation implement things?
7. Implementation - What code expresses all of the above?

The Central Thesis:
"The future of software engineering belongs to organisations that treat
specifications, not source code, as their primary intellectual asset."

Why This Matters Now:
1. RFCs proved it works - 50 years of internet history
2. AI makes it economical - Implementation now takes minutes, not weeks
3. Knowledge is durable - Technology changes, business knowledge persists

GETTING STARTED WITH IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

Step 1: Choose a small feature to specify
Step 2: Write a use case (what happens step by step)
Step 3: Create a domain model (what entities exist)
Step 4: Document architecture constraints (if needed)
Step 5: Ask AI to implement from the specification
Step 6: Review and validate
Step 7: Deploy
Step 8: Iterate based on learning

Expected timeline:
- First feature: 4-6 weeks (learning curve)
- Second feature: 2-3 weeks
- Third feature: 1-2 weeks
- By fifth feature: consistent rhythm, high confidence

ROI appears:
- At 3 months: 1.5x productivity gain
- At 6 months: 2x productivity gain
- At 1 year: 4x productivity gain
- At 2 years: 8x productivity gain (includes technology changes)
